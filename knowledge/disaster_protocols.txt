# CROWDSHIELD AI - DISASTER RESPONSE PROTOCOLS

[PROTOCOL DR-01: STAMPEDE PREVENTION]
Trigger: Crowd pressure > 6.0 in any single zone for more than 30 seconds.
Actions:
  1. QRT deploys in a human-chain formation at the choke point.
  2. PA system plays: "Please stop and wait calmly. Move slowly to your left. Assistance is on the way."
  3. Security control room activates CCTV tracking on affected zone.
  4. Route additional security personnel from standby positions within 2 minutes.
  5. If pressure does not drop within 3 minutes, escalate to Phase 3 evacuation.

[PROTOCOL DR-02: SURGE / RAPID INFLOW]
Trigger: Crowd count increases by > 30% in under 60 seconds in any zone.
Actions:
  1. Close secondary entry gates immediately.
  2. Implement single-file queuing at primary entry with crowd meters.
  3. Alert Operations Control Room and request QRT standby.
  4. Monitor optical flow velocity; if velocity drops below 0.2 m/s, trigger DR-01.

[PROTOCOL DR-03: CHOKE POINT / BOTTLENECK]
Trigger: High density (> 5 persons per sq metre) combined with low velocity (< 0.15 m/s).
Actions:
  1. Dispatch a clearance team to identify and remove physical obstructions.
  2. Redirect at least 50% of crowd to alternative exits using dynamic LED signs.
  3. Halt all incoming traffic to affected corridor.
  4. Maintain radio contact with clearance team every 60 seconds.

[PROTOCOL DR-04: AUDIO ALARM / PANIC DETECTION]
Trigger: Ambient audio level exceeds threshold AND crowd pressure > 5.0.
Actions:
  1. Do NOT activate sirens (amplifies panic). Use calm PA voice only.
  2. Increase lighting in affected area by 20% to reduce disorientation.
  3. Deploy marshals in high-visibility gear to act as visual anchors.
  4. Activate CrowdShield AI advisory copilot for real-time guidance.

[PROTOCOL DR-05: ESCALATOR / INFRASTRUCTURE FAILURE]
Trigger: Any escalator or structural failure reported or detected.
Actions:
  1. Stop all escalators immediately via the master control switch.
  2. Post marshals at top and bottom of each disabled escalator.
  3. Redirect traffic to stairways; increase capacity by removing temporary barriers.
  4. Notify maintenance team and log the incident in CrowdShield AI.

[COMMUNICATION HIERARCHY]
Level 1 – Zone Marshal → Sector Supervisor (< 1 min)
Level 2 – Sector Supervisor → Operations Control Room (< 2 min)
Level 3 – Operations Control Room → Incident Commander (< 3 min)
Level 4 – Incident Commander → Emergency Services (immediate if life-threatening)
